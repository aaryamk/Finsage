---
name: Projection assumptions
description: Why FinSage exposes unfunded SIP cash flow and does not invent maturity dates for existing loans.
---

Preserve the supplied annual SIP step-up while deducting every contribution from available net-worth cash flow. Negative projected liquid cash must not be silently erased or replaced with invented salary growth.

**Why:** The PRD mandates stepped-up contributions but supplies no salary-growth assumption. Ignoring the funding requirement would create assets from money the persona never received.

**How to apply:** When extending projections, distinguish hypothetical contribution schedules from sustainable cash flow. Document any future income-growth assumption explicitly.

Treat existing EMI commitments as ongoing until the product provides their principal, interest and maturity details.

**Why:** A monthly payment amount alone cannot determine a defensible repayment schedule.

**How to apply:** Do not infer existing-loan balances or payoff dates from EMI alone; the simulator's newly specified home loan can be amortised normally.
